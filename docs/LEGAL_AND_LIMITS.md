# Legal and project limits

## Law cited in the agreement

The project agreement cites Chile's Law 21.719 as an example of the kind of requirement an organization may need to address when it must show who accessed information, under what permission, and for what purpose. The agreement says the primary legal text was not read for this project run and the implementation was not checked against it. It expressly says Farolero does not comply with that law or any other law. The law is context for the problem, not a compliance claim or legal interpretation by this repository.

No competent legal professional has reviewed the project materials. This document is not legal advice and does not present a legal opinion.

## What the project does not claim

- It does not claim compliance with Law 21.719 or any other law or standard.
- It does not claim that a local receipt establishes legal identity, a person's authority, the truth of an input, or legal admissibility.
- It does not claim adoption, permission, or endorsement by any institution or third party.
- It does not claim that the system is a finished product or ready for use.
- It does not process real personal, health, financial, or third-party data in this project run. All sample data and agents are fictional.

## Project limits

The effect is a local, reversible write to a project file. No network, blockchain, payment, testnet anchor, or institutional integration is active. Farolero consumes the Vespi kernel and does not modify it. The code is not included in this public repository today. The suite's captured result and the pending kernel refix are described in [`EVIDENCE.md`](EVIDENCE.md).

## Open questions

- What legal and organizational requirements would apply to any real deployment and its data?
- What evidence would a responsible organization need to review before granting an agent authority over a real system?
- What review would be needed before describing any future implementation as compliant with a law or standard?
- How should permission, revocation, receipt retention, and human review be handled in a real institutional setting?

These are open questions. This repository does not answer them as legal conclusions.
